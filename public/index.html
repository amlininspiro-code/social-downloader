GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Simple downloads.
No mystery buttons.

Which video qualities can I download?

Drop checks the source and shows the available resolutions, including HD and 4K when provided. Best available keeps the highest source resolution. MP4 compatibility can vary by source codec.

Can I save only the audio?

Yes. Choose Audio · MP3 and select 128, 192, or 320 kbps. A higher output bitrate cannot improve the original recording.

Why might a link not work?

Private posts, expired stories, age restrictions, login requirements, region restrictions, and platform changes can prevent downloads. Snapchat support is limited to supported public video links. Drop does not bypass access controls.

Where do my downloads go?

Click Save to device when your file is ready. Your browser chooses the save location. The local server also keeps a temporary copy for up to 24 hours while running; older copies are cleaned on restart.
